✦ PERSONALITY

Quiet, observant and difficult to read. Vex rarely reveals what he is thinking, but he notices more than he lets anyone see. He carries himself with patience and precision, always watching, always listening, and often understanding the truth before the others do.

🗝️ SECRET KEEPER'S NOTE

“Some truths are not meant to be spoken the moment they are discovered. I keep what must be protected, not because I want to hide the truth, but because I know when the time has come to reveal it.”

✦ MEMBER LORE

Vex comes from Hollow, a world where silence carries more meaning than words. From an early age, he learned to listen to what others overlooked — the pauses between sentences, the secrets hidden behind a smile, and the truths people were not yet ready to face.

When the paths of the seven stars began to converge, Vex did not ask questions. He simply watched, listened and remembered. Every encounter became another piece of a story that only he seemed able to see in its entirety.

He became AETHER's Secret Keeper — the one trusted with memories, promises and truths that cannot yet be spoken aloud.

Some secrets are meant to be revealed. Others are meant to be protected.

📜 HOLLOW

Hollow is a world where light has almost completely disappeared. Its skies are silent, its landscapes are filled with shadows, and its secrets are rarely revealed willingly.

Vex learned early that darkness does not always announce itself. Sometimes it simply waits, watches and slowly grows.

His black panther Guardian has remained beside him through every shadow, carrying a purple crystal that reflects the light Hollow could not destroy.

But Hollow has not forgotten Vex. And somewhere within its darkness, something is still trying to call him home.
