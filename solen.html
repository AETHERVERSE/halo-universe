✦ PERSONALITY

Calm, steady and deeply protective. Solen naturally takes responsibility for the people around him. He doesn't lead by demanding attention — he leads by being the person everyone can rely on when the stars begin to fall.

📜 SOLARION

Solarion is a world shaped by light, warmth and endless skies. It is where Solen learned that leadership is not about standing above others — but standing beside them.

His golden Guardian appeared when the first light of a new day touched the horizon. Since then, the lion has remained at his side, protecting the light entrusted to him.

But even the brightest light casts a shadow. Somewhere beyond Solarion, Hollow is watching.
