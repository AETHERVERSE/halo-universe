📜 VOLTARIS

Voltaris is a world where energy never truly sleeps. Storms move across its skies, lightning runs through its landscapes and every heartbeat seems connected to the pulse of the planet.

Kairo embodies that energy. He is movement when everything feels still, laughter when the atmosphere becomes heavy and the spark that pulls the others forward.

His wolf Guardian runs beside him through the storms, carrying the Blitzkern — a relic charged with the raw energy of Voltaris.

But even endless energy can be exhausted. Somewhere beyond the storms, Hollow waits for the moment when Kairo's light begins to flicker.
