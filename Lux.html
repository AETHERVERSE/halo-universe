📜 LUMENIA

Lumenia is a world where light is gentle rather than overwhelming. Its skies glow with countless stars, and its cities are surrounded by peaceful, warm radiance.

Lux carries that feeling wherever he goes. His greatest strength is not simply his kindness, but his ability to make others feel safe enough to be themselves.

His white cat Guardian, Lumen, follows quietly beside him, carrying the Star Pendant — a relic that reflects the purest light of Lumenia.

But even the gentlest light can be threatened. Hollow knows that comfort can become a weakness if someone learns how to turn it into fear.
