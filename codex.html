✦ THE SEVEN WORLDS

Seven worlds. Seven stars. Seven origins. Each world carries its own light, its own memories, and a story waiting to be remembered.

☀️ SOLARION

The World of Light

A world of golden skies, radiant cities and endless warmth. Solarion is a place where light is more than a force — it is a promise that darkness will never have the final word.

It is the home world of Solen, the first light among the seven stars.

🖤 HOLLOW

The World of Shadows

A silent world hidden beneath endless darkness. Forgotten truths linger between ancient ruins, while shadows conceal things that were never meant to be remembered.

Hollow is the home world of Vex — and a place whose secrets reach far deeper than anyone knows.

⚡ VOLTARIS

The World of Storms

A world driven by lightning, powerful storms and endless movement. Energy flows through its cities, its skies and everything that calls Voltaris home.

It is the home world of Kairo, whose spirit burns with the same restless energy.

🌊 THALASSIA

The World of Tides

An ocean world where vast seas hold memories older than the stars. Its tides carry stories across generations, never truly letting the past disappear.

Thalassia is the home world of Riven.

🔥 IGNIS

The World of Fire

A world of volcanic landscapes, burning skies and fierce inner strength. Beneath its heat lies a warmth that can still be found even in the darkest moments.

It is the home world of Zenith.

✨ LUMENIA

The World of Gentle Light

A peaceful world filled with quiet stars, crystalline cities and soft, endless light. Lumenia is a place where weary hearts can finally rest.

It is the home world of Lux.

🌙 NOCTEUM

The World of Endless Night

A mysterious world beneath deep blue skies, where ancient secrets hide among the stars. Nocteum is quiet, distant and beautiful in a way that can only be understood after the darkness settles.

It is the home world of Nero.
