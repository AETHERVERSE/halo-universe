📜 IGNIS

Ignis is a world shaped by fire. Volcanoes rise beneath crimson skies, rivers of molten light flow through the land, and warmth can be found even in the most dangerous places.

Zenith carries that warmth with him. His ability to make people laugh and feel comfortable is more than just personality — it is part of the light he carries.

His Phoenix Guardian, Pyra, rises from the flames beside him, carrying the Flameheart — a relic said to contain the eternal fire of Ignis.

But fire can illuminate or consume. Hollow knows this better than anyone.
