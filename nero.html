📜 NOCTEUM

Nocteum is a world where night never truly ends. Its skies are deep blue, filled with distant stars, and its quiet landscapes seem to hide countless stories beneath the darkness.

Nero is the youngest of AETHER, but being the youngest does not mean seeing the least. He notices details others overlook and often understands more than he lets anyone know.

His Raven Guardian, Noctis, follows him through the endless night, carrying the Nightfall Pendant — a relic connected to the deepest darkness of Nocteum.

Hollow is drawn to Nocteum because darkness recognizes darkness. But Nero's light is not the kind that needs to be loud to survive.
